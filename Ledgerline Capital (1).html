Our approach

How we trade, and how we limit losses

Strategies

Scheduled buying
Fixed amounts are bought at regular times, so no single price decides the entry.
Portfolio rebalancing
When one coin grows past its target share, we sell part and restore the original balance.
Active trading
Short-term positions based on price trends, always opened with a pre-set exit.

Risk controls

Stop-loss on active trades
Each position has a maximum loss set before it opens.
Position size limits
No single trade can use more than a fixed share of your balance.
Separate client records
Every account has its own ledger so balances can be checked line by line.
Questions

Frequently asked

Is Ledgerline Capital a real company?

No. It is a fictional brand built for a course assignment to show how an investment site could look. It does not hold funds or trade for anyone.

Are returns guaranteed?

No investment return is guaranteed. A real provider that promises fixed or risk-free profit from Bitcoin trading should be treated as a warning sign.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Bitcoin prices can fall sharply and you can lose part or all of what you invest. Only invest money you can afford to lose.
